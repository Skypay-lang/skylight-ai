# SKYLIGHT AI 🌍

> African Intelligence, Built for the World.

SKYLIGHT AI is an African multilingual AI initiative founded by Abubakar Salisu Kiyawa, FSK.

We are starting with Hausa and English, with a long-term vision of building AI technology that understands African languages, cultures, businesses, and users.

## Vision

To build AI that understands Africa, speaks Africa, and solves African problems.

## Initial Focus

- Hausa AI
- English AI
- Hausa ↔ English translation
- AI assistant
- African language technology
- Voice AI
- Business AI

## Roadmap

Nigeria 🇳🇬 → Africa 🌍 → Global 🌎
